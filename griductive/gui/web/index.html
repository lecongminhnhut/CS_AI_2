    CASE INSTRUCTIONS

    How to play

    1. Tap a revealed card to spotlight its clue. Each clue has two to four underlined terms. Matching cards glow in the colors of the terms that refer to them.
    2. Tap an underlined term to focus its references. Tap it again to restore all highlights.
    3. Tap a hidden character to choose Innocent or Criminal. A verdict needs proof from the visible clues.
    4. A proved verdict reveals another clue. Hint highlights the evidence, Reveal points to a suspect, and Clear removes the highlights.
